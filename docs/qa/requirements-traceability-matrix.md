## Notes on coverage gaps (honest)

- **22 recording-coverage warnings** in `traceability-report.md` are by design:
  recordings illustrate a representative subset (6 clips). Every FR is decided by
  automated tests and/or an eval case, not by a clip. No FR is unproven.
- **NFR-PERF-01/02** are observed locally, not asserted by a hard millisecond
  timer in CI — treated as met by design (`force-dynamic` + pure series seam,
  ≤365-pt tests) but see risk R-05.
- Deploy-gated NFRs (PERF-03, COMPAT-03, SEC-02, LOC-02) are **Future** — out of
  MVP scope per ADR-0001 (no cloud deploy).
