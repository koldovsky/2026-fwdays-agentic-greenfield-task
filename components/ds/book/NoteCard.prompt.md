A single color-coded reading note — the heart of Bookshelf.

```jsx
<NoteCard
  color="coral"
  excerpt="The opposite of love is not hate, it's indifference."
  note="Connects to Fromm — attention as the real currency of care."
  page={88}
  tags={['ethics', 're-read']}
  links={3}
/>
```

`color` is a highlighter key. `excerpt` is painted in that color; `note` is your reflection. `links` shows the cross-reference count.
