# Slots Specification

## Purpose

The `slots` capability computes, ranks, and holds trial-lesson time slots. The
schedule source of truth is the DEMO Google Calendar (TC-CAL-01, ADR-0003):
free slots are the deterministic Mon–Fri grid generated in code **minus** the
calendar's busy intervals — the calendar can only remove options, never add
them. A pure `rankSlots()` function orders the free slots; the agent proposes
only the top-ranked 2–3 from the code-vetted list. Choosing a slot creates a
soft hold (`pending`) backed by a tentative calendar event. This capability
owns FR-SLOT-01..04 and guardrail FR-GUARD-03.

### Conventions used throughout this spec

These three conventions are not separately-numbered requirements — they are
the shared vocabulary every requirement and scenario below relies on for an
objective pass/fail decision:

- **Timezone.** All grid times, lead-facing times, and scenario examples in
  this spec are **Europe/Kyiv wall-clock time** (EET/EEST per the IANA tz
  database; the offset shifts on DST transition days, the wall-clock grid
  does not — a Mon–Fri day always has starts 10:00–19:00 Kyiv time). The
  Google Calendar free/busy API returns RFC3339 timestamps with an explicit
  UTC offset; the calendar adapter SHALL convert every such timestamp to
  Europe/Kyiv wall-clock time before it is compared against the grid or
  against any interval in this spec. No comparison between a grid slot and a
  calendar interval is ever made in raw UTC.
- **Interval semantics (half-open).** A slot occupies `[start, start+60min)`
  and a busy interval occupies `[busyStart, busyEnd)`, both half-open. A slot
  is disqualified iff `slot.start < busy.end AND busy.start < slot.end`.
  Touching boundaries do **not** overlap: a slot that starts exactly when a
  busy interval ends, or ends exactly when a busy interval begins, is free.
- **Proposal horizon.** The grid is generated and free/busy is fetched for
  the **next 14 calendar days** from the moment of computation. "The entire
  proposal horizon" in this spec always means this 14-day window; a scenario
  that needs "the calendar is fully busy" means fully busy across all 14
  days.
## Requirements
### Requirement: Deterministic slot grid bounds all offers

The slot grid SHALL be generated by deterministic code in `lib/`: Mon–Fri
only, 60-minute slots with start times from 10:00 through 19:00 inclusive,
evaluated in Europe/Kyiv wall-clock time (see Conventions above)
(BC-SCHEDULE-01). The agent SHALL never offer a slot outside this grid; the
model only picks from the code-provided list, and the calendar can only
subtract from the grid, never add to it (FR-GUARD-03).

#### Scenario: Grid contains only Mon–Fri 10:00–19:00 starts

- GIVEN the grid generator is called for any date range
- WHEN the generated slots are inspected
- THEN every slot is 60 minutes long, starts on the hour between 10:00 and 19:00 inclusive Europe/Kyiv time, and falls on Monday through Friday
- AND no Saturday or Sunday slot is ever generated

#### Scenario: Weekend or after-hours request is refused with grid-bounded alternatives

- GIVEN a lead explicitly asks for a Saturday lesson or a 21:00 lesson
- WHEN the agent responds
- THEN the reply offers no slot outside Mon–Fri 10:00–19:00 starts
- AND the reply is in Ukrainian and states that the school books Mon–Fri 10:00–20:00, offering in-grid alternatives instead

> Ownership note (S1 review-gate): the grid guarantee (no out-of-grid slot can
> ever be offered) is implemented and tested in this capability; the Ukrainian
> refusal *message composition* is conversation-layer behavior delivered by
> `intake` (S2), which consumes this capability's code-vetted list. The
> scenario is verified end-to-end once S2 lands.

#### Scenario: Calendar data cannot add slots outside the grid

- GIVEN the calendar adapter returns a free interval on Sunday (or any interval outside the grid) in fixture data
- WHEN free slots are computed
- THEN no slot outside the deterministic grid appears in the result — subtraction is the only operation applied to the grid

### Requirement: Free slots are computed as grid minus calendar busy minus pending holds

The system SHALL compute free slots as the deterministic grid minus busy
intervals fetched on demand from the DEMO Google Calendar's free/busy API,
and SHALL additionally exclude slots held by other leads (`pending` bookings,
including their tentative calendar events) from every offer. The agent SHALL
propose 2–3 free slots that satisfy BC-SCHEDULE-01 and the lead's stated
weekday/time preferences (FR-SLOT-01).

#### Scenario: Busy interval removes overlapping grid slots

- GIVEN the calendar reports a busy interval 11:30–13:30 Europe/Kyiv time on a Tuesday
- WHEN free slots for that Tuesday are computed
- THEN the 11:00, 12:00, and 13:00 slots are absent from the result (per the half-open overlap rule in Conventions: `slot.start < busy.end AND busy.start < slot.end`)
- AND the 10:00 and 14:00–19:00 slots remain free

#### Scenario: A slot touching a busy interval's boundary is not disqualified

- GIVEN the calendar reports a busy interval 12:00–14:00 Europe/Kyiv time on a Wednesday
- WHEN free slots for that Wednesday are computed
- THEN the 11:00 slot (ends exactly when the busy interval begins) and the 14:00 slot (starts exactly when the busy interval ends) are both present in the result
- AND the 12:00 and 13:00 slots, fully inside the busy interval, are absent

#### Scenario: Slots held by other leads are excluded from offers

- GIVEN lead A holds Wednesday 15:00 (`pending` booking with a tentative calendar event)
- WHEN lead B with matching preferences is offered slots
- THEN Wednesday 15:00 does not appear in lead B's offer, regardless of ranking

#### Scenario: Agent proposes two to three preference-matching slots

- GIVEN a lead completed intake with preferences "Tuesday or Thursday, after 16:00" and at least three matching slots are free
- WHEN the agent proposes slots
- THEN the proposal contains exactly 2 or 3 slots, each on Tuesday or Thursday with a start time of 16:00 or later, each present in the code-computed free list

### Requirement: A lead-named concrete date is honored within the grid

When the lead names a concrete or relative day, the agent SHALL resolve it to a
calendar date (e.g. "завтра", "у пʼятницю", "14 липня") and pass it to slot
proposal alongside the weekday/time preferences (FR-SLOT-01). Deterministic
code — never the model — decides whether to honor it: the date is accepted
only when it is well-formed (YYYY-MM-DD) and within the proposal horizon
[today, today+14] (Conventions), and slots are then computed for that single
day from the same Mon–Fri grid, using the lead's stated time window when valid
and the full teaching day (10:00–20:00) otherwise. A date that is malformed or
out of range is ignored and the ordinary weekday-preference path is used
instead. A weekend or already-past named day yields no grid slots (the Mon–Fri
grid has none), so the lead takes the kind "no free times" / weekend-refusal
path — the grid guarantee (FR-GUARD-03) is never bypassed by a lead-named date.

#### Scenario: Lead names a specific in-grid date

- GIVEN today is a weekday and the lead asks to book "завтра" (the next day, itself a weekday) and that day has free grid slots
- WHEN the agent proposes slots with the code-vetted resolved date
- THEN every proposed slot falls on exactly that date, within Mon–Fri 10:00–19:00 starts, drawn from the code-computed free list
- AND the lead's stated time window narrows the day when valid, otherwise the full teaching day is offered

#### Scenario: Lead names a weekend day

- GIVEN the lead asks to book on a Saturday (or any Sat/Sun date)
- WHEN slots are computed for that date
- THEN no slot is produced — the Mon–Fri grid has none for that day — and the lead receives the Ukrainian Mon–Fri redirect, never an out-of-grid offer (BC-SCHEDULE-01)

#### Scenario: Out-of-range or malformed date falls back to weekday preferences

- GIVEN the resolved date is malformed or falls outside [today, today+14]
- WHEN slot proposal runs
- THEN the named date is ignored and slots are proposed from the lead's weekday/time preferences across the proposal horizon, exactly as when no date was named

### Requirement: Slots are ranked by a pure rankSlots() function

Slot ordering SHALL be produced by a pure `rankSlots()` function in `lib/`
(framework-free, unit-testable per TC-PURE-01) applying, in priority order:
(1) fit to the lead's preferred weekdays and time window, (2) teacher
convenience — minimal schedule fragmentation, i.e. adjacency to existing
calendar events and no isolated gaps, (3) earlier dates first. The agent
SHALL present only the top-ranked 2–3 slots and SHALL NOT reorder, add to,
or substitute the code-vetted list (FR-SLOT-04).

#### Scenario: Preference fit dominates ranking

- GIVEN a lead prefers Monday mornings, and both a Monday 10:00 slot and a Friday 10:00 slot are free
- WHEN `rankSlots()` is called with the lead's preferences
- THEN the Monday 10:00 slot ranks above the Friday 10:00 slot

#### Scenario: Teacher convenience breaks ties among equally fitting slots

- GIVEN two free slots equally fit the lead's preferences, one adjacent to an existing calendar event and one that would leave an isolated one-hour gap
- WHEN `rankSlots()` is called
- THEN the adjacent slot ranks above the gap-creating slot

#### Scenario: Earlier dates break remaining ties

- GIVEN two free slots identical in preference fit and teacher convenience, on different dates
- WHEN `rankSlots()` is called
- THEN the slot on the earlier date ranks higher

#### Scenario: rankSlots() is pure and deterministic

- GIVEN the same inputs (free slots, lead preferences, existing busy intervals)
- WHEN `rankSlots()` is called repeatedly in a unit test with no I/O available
- THEN it returns the identical ordering every time and performs no network, DB, or SDK calls

#### Scenario: Agent proposal is drawn only from the top of the ranked list

- GIVEN `rankSlots()` returned an ordered list of five free slots
- WHEN the agent composes its slot proposal
- THEN every proposed slot is among the top 3 of the ranked list, and no slot outside the ranked list appears in the message

### Requirement: Choosing a slot creates a soft hold with a tentative calendar event

When the lead picks one of the offered slots, the booking SHALL move to
`pending` and deterministic code (not the model) SHALL create a **tentative**
event in the DEMO calendar, making the hold visible to the teacher and
blocking the interval via free/busy. There SHALL be no automatic hold expiry
in MVP — a stale hold is resolved only by the administrator's Confirm or
Decline (FR-SLOT-02).

#### Scenario: Lead picks a slot

- GIVEN the agent proposed slots and the lead chooses one of them
- WHEN the hold is executed
- THEN the booking status becomes `pending` in SQLite
- AND a tentative event for exactly that slot's interval exists in the DEMO calendar, created by deterministic hold code
- AND subsequent free-slot computations no longer offer that slot to any lead

#### Scenario: Holds never expire automatically

- GIVEN a booking has been `pending` for any length of time with no admin decision
- WHEN time passes (hours or days)
- THEN the booking remains `pending`, the tentative event remains in the calendar, and no scheduled job or timer changes either

#### Scenario: Lead picks a slot that a manual calendar event has just taken (hold collision)

- GIVEN the agent offered a slot, and the teacher manually added a calendar event over that interval after the offer was computed
- WHEN the hold code re-checks free/busy before creating the tentative event and detects the collision
- THEN no tentative event is created and the booking does not move to `pending`
- AND the agent tells the lead (in Ukrainian) that this time was just taken and offers the next best free slots from a freshly computed, re-ranked list

#### Scenario: Two leads pick the same offered slot concurrently (hold race)

- GIVEN lead A and lead B were both offered the same free slot, and lead A's hold code runs first, successfully creating a tentative event and moving lead A's booking to `pending`
- WHEN lead B's hold code then re-checks free/busy before creating its own tentative event, and finds lead A's now-existing tentative event occupying the interval
- THEN lead B's booking does NOT move to `pending` and no tentative event is created for lead B — this is the same code path as the manual-event collision above, exercised under a race between two leads rather than a manual edit
- AND lead A's `pending` hold and tentative event are unaffected
- AND lead B receives the "this time was just taken" message (Ukrainian) with the next best free slots from a freshly computed, re-ranked list

### Requirement: Fewer than two matches widens the window explicitly

The system SHALL widen the search when fewer than 2 free slots match the
lead's stated preferred weekdays/time window, through the following
deterministic, ordered steps, stopping at the first step whose cumulative
candidate pool reaches 2 free slots:

1. **Step 1 — time widening (same weekdays).** Extend the preferred time
   window by 60 minutes on both ends (earlier start, later end), clipped to
   the grid's 10:00–19:00 start bounds, keeping the same preferred weekdays.
2. **Step 2 — day widening (adjacent weekdays).** Using the Step 1 time
   window, add the weekday(s) immediately adjacent, within Mon–Fri, to each
   preferred weekday — Monday's only Mon–Fri neighbor is Tuesday, Friday's
   only neighbor is Thursday, a mid-week day gets both neighbors.
3. **Step 3 — full grid.** If Steps 1–2 still total fewer than 2 free slots,
   fall back to the top-ranked free slots from the entire Mon–Fri grid across
   the proposal horizon (Conventions), regardless of preference.

The proposal is composed by taking candidates from the narrowest step first
and filling any remaining seats from the next step only if needed, until 2–3
slots are selected; `rankSlots()` orders candidates within each step
(FR-SLOT-03). Whenever any offered slot came from Step 1, 2, or 3 rather than
the lead's original stated window, the agent SHALL say so explicitly, in
Ukrainian — the lead never receives an empty or silent result.

#### Scenario: One matching slot triggers Step 1 (time) widening

- GIVEN the lead's stated preference is Tuesday 10:00–11:00 (matching only the Tuesday 10:00 slot) and the Tuesday 11:00 slot is also free
- WHEN the agent proposes slots
- THEN Step 1 widens the window to Tuesday starts 10:00–12:00 (±60 minutes, clipped to the grid), which now matches both Tuesday 10:00 and Tuesday 11:00
- AND the proposal contains exactly these 2 slots, with no Step 2 or Step 3 candidates needed
- AND the message explicitly states that the offer includes a widened window (e.g. "у бажаний час вільне лише одне вікно, тож додаю сусідній варіант")

#### Scenario: Zero matching slots resolved by Step 2 (day) widening

- GIVEN the lead's stated preference is Tuesday 10:00–11:00 only, the Tuesday 10:00 slot is busy (zero matches), and after Step 1 (Tuesday starts 10:00–12:00) the Tuesday 11:00 slot is also busy (zero matches remain), while Monday 10:00 and Wednesday 10:00 — Tuesday's Mon–Fri neighbors — are free
- WHEN the agent proposes slots
- THEN Step 2 adds Monday and Wednesday to the Step 1 time window, matching Monday 10:00 and Wednesday 10:00
- AND the proposal contains exactly these 2 slots
- AND the message explicitly states that the offer is outside the lead's originally preferred weekday

#### Scenario: No free slots even after widening through the full grid

- GIVEN the calendar is fully busy across the entire 14-day proposal horizon (Conventions), so Steps 1–3 all yield zero free slots
- WHEN the agent responds
- THEN the reply explicitly says no free times are currently available and that the administrator will follow up with options
- AND the request is preserved with the collected preferences so the administrator can propose a time (FR-HITL-01 "Propose another time")
- AND the lead never receives an empty slot list without explanation

### Requirement: Calendar API failure degrades deterministically

The system SHALL respond to any Google Calendar API failure (free/busy fetch
or tentative-event creation) with the deterministic Ukrainian
apology-and-retry message defined by NFR-REL-01 — composed without LLM
involvement — SHALL preserve the conversation and booking state for
resumption, and SHALL never leave the data stores inconsistent or silently
drop the lead's message.

#### Scenario: Free/busy fetch fails while proposing slots

- GIVEN the lead is ready for slot proposals and the calendar free/busy call fails (timeout, 5xx, or auth error)
- WHEN the failure is caught
- THEN the lead receives the deterministic Ukrainian apology-and-retry message, produced without an LLM call
- AND the conversation state (intake data, booking in `proposing`) is preserved so the lead's next message resumes slot proposal
- AND no slots are invented or offered from stale assumptions

#### Scenario: Tentative-event creation fails during a hold

- GIVEN the lead picked a slot and the calendar event-creation call fails
- WHEN the failure is caught
- THEN the booking does NOT move to `pending` (no hold exists without its tentative event)
- AND the lead receives the deterministic Ukrainian apology-and-retry message
- AND the lead's slot choice is preserved so retrying the hold requires no re-selection

## Out of scope (explicit exclusions)

The following are intentionally unsupported in MVP — do not report them as
bugs:

- **Automatic hold expiry**: a stale `pending` hold is resolved only by the administrator (FR-SLOT-02).
- **Calendar push/webhooks**: free/busy is polled on demand only; no inbound connections (NFR-LOCAL-01, TC-CAL-01).
- **Multi-teacher scheduling**: exactly one teacher, one DEMO calendar.
- **Rescheduling of already-confirmed lessons** and reminders.
- **Confirming a booking**: the `confirmed` transition belongs to `booking-hitl` (FR-HITL-01..04, FR-GUARD-01); this capability only holds slots.
- **Saturday/Sunday or outside 10:00–20:00 offers** — never, even on explicit lead request (BC-SCHEDULE-01).
