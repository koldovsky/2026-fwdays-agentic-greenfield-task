- [ ] 5.2 `POST /api/timer/stop` — save-modal payload (notes + final category) -> persisted session; `201` — FR-TIMER-03
- [ ] 5.3 `POST /api/timer/discard` — confirmed discard -> undo token; persists nothing — FR-TIMER-04
